Trading rules in this version

  • ES3 only; paper starting capital S$20,000.
  • 100-share lots; no automatic averaging down.
  • All executions are entered manually by you.
  • STI trend is recorded as context, not treated as a standalone buy/sell signal.
  • No live data feed, automated order placement, or automatic chart interpretation.
  • Performance calculations are estimates and depend on the prices and fees entered.
